Automated Client Response System

Professional Services Firm

The Problem

Team spent a large share of the day answering repetitive client queries across email and WhatsApp. Response times stretched across the working day. Clients complained about slow turnaround.

What I Built

Built an n8n workflow that routes incoming queries, drafts context-aware responses using AI, and sends them through approved channels. Human reviews only edge cases.

What I Worked On

  • Mapped 14 repetitive query categories across email and WhatsApp
  • Drafted 6 reusable response templates with human-review escalation rules
  • Handed off a runbook plus a 2-person training session for the ops team
Delivered in 2 weeks

Frictionless Service Purchase Flow

Consulting & Advisory Practice

The Problem

Buyers abandoned checkout because intake forms duplicated sales calls. Conversion stalled under 4%.

What I Built

Replaced the static intake with a conversational qualifier that captured the same fields, surfaced pricing, and booked a discovery call only when intent was real.

What I Worked On

  • Drafted 9 qualifying questions aligned to existing discovery framework
  • Wired Stripe checkout + Google Calendar booking behind a single flow
  • Cut form completion time from 11 to 3 minutes; conversions rose 3.2×
Built in 9 days

Automated Meeting-to-Action Pipeline

Operations Team

The Problem

Every client meeting produced 40+ minutes of notes nobody read. Action items surfaced days later in standup, often misattributed.

What I Built

Built a recording-to-action pipeline: transcript → summary → tasks → CRM. Each step had a human approval gate.

What I Worked On

  • Integrated 3 video platforms with a single transcript service
  • Designed approval gates so the team could correct attribution before sync
  • Action items now reach CRM within 4 hours of meeting end

What this is NOT for

I'd rather lose a sale than take on work I can't deliver. If these describe you, there are better options.

Not for enterprise transformation programs

I work with founders, operators, and small teams — not large organizations running the 2023 playbook with better tools. That's the dabbling trap. If you need a vendor to manage your AI rollout across 500 employees, other consultants are better suited.

Not for leaders still evaluating whether AI matters

If you're still forming committees to study AI, start elsewhere. I work with people who are ready to execute — not to write reports.

Not for people who want AI to do the work for them

I build systems you understand and operate. If you want AI magic that runs on its own, this isn't the right fit. You need to own the process.

Not if you expect AI to fix team dysfunction

AI amplifies what's already there. If your team struggles with accountability, trust, or decision rights, AI won't fix that. Fix the team first.
